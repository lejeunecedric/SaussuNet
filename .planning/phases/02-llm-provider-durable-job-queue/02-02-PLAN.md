<objective>
Queue-lifecycle slice: a job row lives a full durable life in SQLite — insert → atomic claim → complete, or fail → backoff-gated retry → dead-letter with retained error, and expired claims get reaped back to pending. Proven at the store level with an injected clock (no sleeps, no flake) before any worker exists.

Purpose: SRCC-03's durability guarantees are pure SQL semantics; isolating them here makes the worker plan (02-04) thin wiring.
