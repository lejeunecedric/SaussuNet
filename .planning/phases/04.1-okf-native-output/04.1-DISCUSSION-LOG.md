## Research that changed the analysis (not from the user)

- The canonical OKF spec moved: `GoogleCloudPlatform/open-knowledge-format` is
  deprecated and now points at `open-knowledge-format/open-knowledge-format`,
  whose SPEC.md is **v0.3** and was updated 2026-11-18 — the same day Phase 04.1
  was inserted into the roadmap.
- v0.2 §5.1 / §13.1 supersede the body `# Citations` list in favour of `sources`
  frontmatter + `[^id]` footnotes. ROADMAP criterion 2 and OKF-03 still describe
  the v0.1 numbered-citation form inherited from the B3Wiki exercise → conflict.
- v0.3 §3.2/§11 drop the "frontmatter in every `.md`" requirement, which would
  dissolve the `raw/` conformance problem entirely — but only if v0.3 is targeted.
